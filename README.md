# Take The Lead

Take the Lead is a small CDDA mod that lets you take control of your NPC companion as long you can talk to them.

## How to use

After installing the mod by putting it into the mod folder of the game and add it to a world's modlist, (C)hat with your NPC companion and select the "You take the lead" dialogue option to switch into controlling the NPC companion you were talking to. It's also works on 2-way radio.

## Version where it works

Version 0.G and beyond.
